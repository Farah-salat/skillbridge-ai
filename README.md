## 📋 Overview
**SkillBridge AI** is a lightweight, responsive web application designed to turn undocumented professional experience into verified, employer-ready proof of competency. Built for skilled refugees across Kenya—including Dadaab, Kakuma, Nairobi, and Garissa—the platform bridges the gap when physical academic certificates are lost during displacement or unrecognized locally.

---

## ✨ Key Features
* **3-Step Competency Verification Flow:**
  1. **Experience & Prior Learning Intake:** Captures work history, settlement location, and sector expertise across five core fields (*Software & Cloud Tech*, *Healthcare & Nursing*, *Accounting & Finance*, *Education & Teaching*, and *Supply Chain & Logistics*) without requiring paper certificates.
  2. **Scenario-Based Competency Assessment:** Evaluates practical, real-world problem-solving tailored to the candidate's chosen profession.
  3. **Verifiable Digital Competency Passport:** Maps demonstrated mastery to Kenyan National Qualifications Authority (**KNQA**) benchmark levels, generates a unique Verification ID (e.g., `SB-KE-2026-8492`), and recommends a 1-step bridging certification pathway.
* **Employer Verification Portal & Directory:** Enables hiring partners to enter any candidate's Verification ID to confirm validated skills and KNQA alignment instantly.
* **One-Click Sample Profiles:** Pre-loaded demonstration profiles representing displaced professionals across Kenya for rapid testing and evaluation.
